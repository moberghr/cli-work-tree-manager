# Activity (what work does in the background)

The top bar's **Activity** dot is grey unless a job needs you (`activityAttention`, `state/activity-view.ts`: one resting at GitHub's limit, or one that worked before and now fails — "worked before" from `ActivityWire.lastOk`, each job's last success, which the log keeps apart from the 60 recent runs a long outage fills; a job that never worked here, like Jira without acli, is not set up and stays quiet); its tooltip says what runs now ("Checking pull requests 5/13"). Its panel lists Now, Coming up (schedules with countdowns) and Recent runs with their decisions, each linked to its session, uneventful repeats of a job folded into one row (`collapseRuns`). `core/platform/activity.ts` is a pure in-memory log (`createActivityLog`: `start` → progress/note/done/fail, `skip` (repeats collapse), `schedule` → next/pause/resume), one per `work web`, served at `GET /api/activity` with a throttled `activity-changed` SSE that doesn't drop the sessions cache. Jobs that report: the PR watch (per decision: CI fix asked, review comments handed over, archived, or kept and why — `autoArchiveVerdict`), the PR and Jira lists, idle sleep, and the Clean up scans. The demo keeps one too (`server/demo/demo-activity.ts`). § WHEN adding a background job, give it a run (and a schedule if it repeats), and note each decision it makes with its reason — that's what the panel is for.
